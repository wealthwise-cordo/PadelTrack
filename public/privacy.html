Privacy notice

Last updated 1 November 2026

The short version

PadelTrack does not sell your information, show ads, or use third-party analytics. We collect what we need to run your account and rank your padel, and nothing to build a profile of you for anyone else.

Other players can see your name, level, rating and the matches you share with them. They cannot see your email address, date of birth, gender, country or password.

We use a small number of service providers to host the site, store the database and send email. They are named below.

Who is responsible

The data controller is Cord van Eck, trading as PadelTrack, a sole trader based in the United Kingdom. We are the controller of your personal information under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018).

Address: 10 Electric Boulevard, Battersea, London, SW11 8BT. Email: hello@padeltrack.co.uk

This notice covers the PadelTrack iPhone app and padeltrack.co.uk. There is a separate terms of use.

What we collect

When you create an account and use PadelTrack we collect:

  • Account details: your name, email address and password (stored only in scrambled, hashed form), and the playing level you choose.
  • Profile details: your gender, date of birth and country when you sign up directly, plus any display name, nickname and avatar colour you set.
  • Playing information: matches you record or take part in (players, scores, date and venue), confirmations, your rating and its history, and events and leagues you organise or play in.
  • Usage and technical information: when you sign in, which days you use the app, and reports of errors (the page involved, the time and your account identifier). Our hosting provider also keeps standard server logs, including IP addresses, for security.
  • Notifications: if you turn on notifications, a token for your device so we can send them. It is deleted when you turn notifications off or sign out.
  • Messages: emails we send you, such as password-reset links, and anything you send us.

Information about other people you add is covered in its own section below.

Why we use it

  • To provide PadelTrack: running your account, recording matches, calculating ratings, and running events and leagues. Lawful basis: performing our contract with you (the terms of use).
  • To show your results to other players: rankings only work if the players you play with can see shared results. Lawful basis: contract, and our legitimate interest in running a fair rankings service.
  • To keep PadelTrack secure and working: preventing misuse, fixing errors, and sending essential emails such as password resets. Lawful basis: legitimate interests.
  • To understand and improve PadelTrack: counting sign-ups, activity and which features are used, and which venues to add. We look at this inside PadelTrack only, and only the operator can see it. Lawful basis: legitimate interests.
  • To meet legal obligations: for example, responding to lawful requests.

We do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing that have legal or similarly significant effects. Your rating is calculated automatically from match results, but it is a sporting estimate only.

Who can see your information

  • Other PadelTrack players can see your name, display name and nickname, avatar colour, level and rating, and the matches, results and statistics you share with them. Players in an event or league can see its fixtures and results.
  • Not shown to other players: your date of birth, gender, country, email address and password.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it.

Our service providers are listed under "Sharing and where data lives" below.

Other people you add

When you add a guest to a match or event, we store their name and, if you give one, the level you think they play at. The matches a guest plays in are kept on their guest profile. If PadelTrack verifies a guest so that their ranked matches count before they join, we also keep a rating and rating history for them.

A guest can take all of this over by joining PadelTrack with their invite link, or ask us to remove it by emailing hello@padeltrack.co.uk. Only add people who are happy for you to do so.

What we do not collect or do

  • no advertising, and no advertising identifiers
  • no third-party analytics cookies or tracking
  • no selling of your information
  • no payment card details; PadelTrack is free
  • no passwords in readable form; they are stored hashed

If you email us, we will have whatever you put in the email, so we can reply.

The App Store and Apple

If you get PadelTrack from the Apple App Store, Apple handles your download and your Apple ID. Apple is a separate controller of that information under its own privacy policy. We do not receive your Apple ID or any payment details from Apple.

We can see Apple's own aggregate App Store statistics for the app, such as views and downloads. Apple does not tell us who you are.

How long we keep it

  • Account, profile and playing information: for as long as your account is open.
  • Error reports: up to 90 days.
  • Password-reset links: they expire after 1 hour.
  • Information we must keep for legal reasons: for as long as the law requires.

If you delete your account. You can delete your account at any time under Edit profile on your profile page, or by emailing us. When you do, we straight away erase your email address, password, nickname, gender, date of birth and country, and remove any of your matches still waiting to be confirmed.

Your name stays on matches that were already confirmed, so other players' results and ratings don't change. Your profile can no longer be viewed and you are removed from other players' circles.

Cookies

PadelTrack uses one essential cookie to keep you signed in. It is strictly necessary for the service to work, so it does not need your consent. We do not use advertising or third-party analytics cookies.

Your rights (UK GDPR)

You can ask us to access, correct, erase or restrict the personal information we hold about you, object to how we use it, and receive it in a portable format.

You can delete your account yourself under Edit profile on your profile page. To use any of the other rights, email hello@padeltrack.co.uk. We will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, please contact us first so we can fix it. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), or call 0303 123 1113.

Children

A parent or guardian must create and manage the account of anyone under 13, and accept our terms on their behalf. Players aged 13 to 17 should have a parent or guardian's permission. If you think a child is using PadelTrack without this, contact us and we will deal with it.

Sharing and where data lives

We do not sell your information. Three providers process it for us, under contracts that require them to protect it:

  • Vercel, website hosting
  • Neon, database hosting
  • Resend, sending email

Some of these providers store or process information outside the UK, including in the United States and the European Union. Where they do, the transfer is protected by safeguards recognised under UK law, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the UK–US data bridge.

Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections and hashed passwords. No online service can be completely secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Changes

We will change the date at the top when we update this notice. If we start collecting more personal information, or using it in a new way, we will make that obvious in the app or by email.
